Cairo University - University Housing Eligibility and Application

Eligibility conditions published on Cairo University's student residence page include:
- The student lives outside Greater Cairo according to the applicable map.
- The student is regularly enrolled in a Cairo University bachelor's or licentiate program.
- The student has passed and advanced to the next year without failed courses.
- The student is medically fit.
- The page also lists conditions related to disciplinary history, payment of fees, and regular residence attendance.

Published application steps:
- Submit the designated residence application form, including academic status approved by the faculty.
- Attach a guarantee for residence-fee payment, a certificate from the Social Solidarity office in the student's original place of residence, and three small personal photographs.
- The page says applications are submitted in August and that the residence administration sets the opening and closing dates.
- Check the current academic-year announcement because dates, rules, and fees may change; some details on the published page are old.
