Cairo University - Postgraduate Application Documents

Cairo University publishes application procedures and documents for postgraduate diplomas, master's degrees, and PhDs. Deadlines and some requirements vary by faculty and degree; check the relevant faculty's announcement.

Documents for Egyptian applicants listed on the university page:
- Temporary bachelor's certificate with the required grade for diploma or master's applicants.
- Temporary master's certificate with the required grade for PhD applicants.
- Academic transcript for all years of study.
- Birth certificate or official extract.
- Copy of the national ID card.
- Six 4x6 cm personal photographs.
- Employer approval for enrollment and study.
- Employer approval for study leave if required by the program or faculty regulations.
- Military service status certificate.
- Armed Forces approval for conscripts; Officers Affairs approval for applicants who are military or police officers.

Additional documents for international applicants listed on the university page include:
- Academic qualification equivalent to the qualification recognized by the Supreme Council of Universities.
- Nomination letter from the applicant's embassy stating funding source, academic year, and degree, addressed to the faculty dean.
- Passport valid for one full year.
- Evidence of freedom from AIDS before enrollment and registration.
- One-year internship certificate for applicants in medical fields.
- Additional forms, data sheets, and photographs submitted through the faculty.

Published admission periods differ by faculty and degree and may change annually. Verify dates with the faculty.
