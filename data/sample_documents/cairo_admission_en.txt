Cairo University - Admission Requirements and Documents Published by the Faculty of Archaeology

This document summarizes information published on the official Cairo University Faculty of Archaeology page. The requirements and document list below apply to new students at the Faculty of Archaeology and must not be generalized to every Cairo University faculty.

Faculty admission requirements:
- The faculty accepts General Secondary Certificate graduates from both science and arts tracks, provided they meet the faculty's minimum admission score.
- Diploma holders are accepted only from the Restoration School in Nasr City.
- Graduates of the Archaeological Restoration Institute in Luxor are accepted.
- Some departments may have additional course or aptitude-test requirements.

Documents required for new students applying to the faculty:
- General Secondary Certificate.
- Computerized birth certificate.
- Six personal photographs.
- Copy of the national ID card.
- Copy of the nomination card.
- Form 2 Gond for male students only, with the student's information completed.

International students apply through the Central Administration for International Students: https://www.wafeden.gov.eg/
