Cairo University - Medical Examination Procedures for New Students

According to Cairo University's online medical-check registration service, the published procedures include:

- Medical examinations take place at Cairo University Student Hospital.
- Bring proof of identity (national ID or computerized birth certificate) and a copy of the General Secondary Certificate success form.
- Bring the medical examination form printed from the website.
- Bring the original and two copies of the Fawry payment receipt.
- Bring two personal photographs if uploading a photo to the website was not possible.
- According to the published instructions, students with equivalent certificates and students from IG schools register at Student Hospital and obtain the payment code there.
- According to the published instructions, international students bring the medical form issued by their faculty and a faculty nomination statement, then go to the examination location without website registration.

Official source: Cairo University online medical examination service for new students:
https://medcheck.cu.edu.eg/

Registration and examination dates change by academic year; check the official website before attending.
