Cairo University's official faculties and institutes page lists Engineering, Medicine, Computers and Artificial Intelligence, Pharmacy, Agriculture, Science, Economics and Political Science, Mass Communication, Archaeology, Arts, Commerce, Specific Education, Nursing, Law, Physical Therapy, Dentistry, Veterinary Medicine, Dar Al-Uloom, Early Childhood Education, Regional and Urban Planning, African Postgraduate Studies, Graduate Studies for Statistical Research, the National Cancer Institute, Graduate School of Education, and the National Institute of Laser Enhanced Sciences, along with other institutes and centers.

The university links to faculty-specific programs and new distinguished programs. Admission requirements and fees vary by faculty and program; consult the official program or faculty page.
